# The Summer Between Us

Your best friend. Her brother. His best friend. One reunion weekend—and three familiar hearts you suddenly cannot stop thinking about.

## Before “Begin chapter one”

FOUR OLD FRIENDS. ONE SUMMER. EVERYTHING LEFT UNSAID.

You, Jihyo, her older brother Bang Chan, and his best friend Joshua were inseparable growing up. Then careers, cities, and years got in the way. Now Jihyo has rented a beach house for one reunion weekend—and the familiar group feels different when everybody is older, bolder, and finally willing to admit what they want.

- **You:** The main character. You grew up with all three friends; this weekend, you decide what those bonds become.
- **Jihyo:** Your best friend—and a possible love you never quite knew how to name.
- **Bang Chan:** Jihyo’s older brother, once a familiar presence and now a very distracting one.
- **Joshua:** Bang Chan’s best friend, whose teasing has always hidden how closely he pays attention.

**Your objective:** Reconnect with all three friends, follow the sparks, and decide whether an old friendship becomes a kiss, a slow beginning, or a bond you keep exactly as it is.

A fictional friends-to-lovers beach romance. Everyone is an adult.

## How choices work

- All three romances remain open. Kitchen choices only select helpers for those scenes.
- Choose the order of all three party visits: three choices, then two, then one. Entering a party activity adds no hearts; the response inside it does.
- The two highest chemistry scores determine who calls. Answering adds +2; declining a rescue adds +1 (total +3); requesting a rescue adds +4, then its continuation adds +3 (total +9).
- After all paths rejoin, the two lower-scoring friends fetch drinks; the highest-scoring person confesses.
- Ties resolve Jihyo, then Chan, then Joshua. Non-romance stats do not select a partner.
- Final choice: kiss → More Than Old Friends; take it slowly → The Slow-Burn Beginning; friendship → The Friendship You Chose. Each has three personal variants.
- New story version: start a fresh Book 2 run; older saves and Admin overrides belong to the previous draft.
